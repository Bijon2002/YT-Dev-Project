## 👑 The Lore & Narrative Storyline

1. **The Golden Reign**:
   - The player enters their royal name. You ruled the peaceful kingdom of Solaria in honor and harmony.
2. **The Cataclysmic Siege**:
   - A coalition of 10 Dark Warlords struck without warning. The fortresses fell, armies were shattered, and your kingdom was stolen.
3. **Exile to the Outer Rim**:
   - Stripped of your crown, you were warped thousands of leagues away to the desolate frontier of **Zone 1**.
4. **The Vengeance March**:
   - With your loyal blade and crown restored, you must march through 10 dangerous biomes, rally blue soldiers, defeat all 10 warlord bosses, and reclaim your stolen citadel!

---

## 🗺️ Interactive World Campaign Map

- **Reclamation Map**: Tracks your army's march from the outer frontier to the Royal Capital Citadel.
- **Node Progression**: Clear zones to advance closer to the capital with live distance countdowns.
- **Instant Deployment**: Replay any liberated zone directly from the Campaign Map modal.

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Desktop**: `A` / `D` or `←` / `→`
- **Mobile Touch**: Drag left or right anywhere on the screen
- **Campaign Map**: Click the **Map 🗺️** button in the HUD
- **Audio**: Click the **Audio 🔊** button in the HUD
